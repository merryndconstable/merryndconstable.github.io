## Lab Director

<img src="/images/merryn.jpg" alt="Merryn Constable" width="220">

### Merryn Constable  
Assistant Professor of Psychology  
Northumbria University

I study how cognition operates in real-world social environments. My work focuses on **psychological ownership** — the feeling that something is “mine” — and how it shapes attention, memory, action, and motivation. I am particularly interested in how understanding ownership can help us **design environments that support cognition and motivation**, whether through organisational structures, social contexts, or physical environments.

My research spans fundamental cognitive science and interdisciplinary applications, including **human–robot interaction, AI-supported healthcare training, and collaborative behaviour in shared spaces**. Across these areas, my aim is to translate cognitive theory into insights that improve how people learn, collaborate, and interact with technology and with each other.

Alongside my core research programme, I am passionate about working with the **NHS Organ Donation and Transplantation community**, supporting research-informed practice and helping to strengthen training and communication in this critically important area of healthcare.
