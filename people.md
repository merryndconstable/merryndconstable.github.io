### Merryn Constable  
Assistant Professor of Psychology  
Northumbria University

<br style="clear:both;">

I study how cognition operates in real-world social environments. My work focuses on psychological ownership, the feeling that something is “mine”, and how it shapes attention, memory, action, and motivation. I am particularly interested in how understanding ownership can inform the design of environments that support cognition and motivation, including organisational structures, social contexts, and physical environments.

My research spans fundamental cognitive science and interdisciplinary applications, including human–robot interaction, AI-supported healthcare training, and collaborative behaviour in shared spaces. Across these areas, I translate cognitive theory into insights that improve how people learn, collaborate, and interact with technology and with each other.

Alongside my core research programme, I work with the NHS Organ Donation and Transplantation community to support research-informed practice and strengthen training and communication in this area of healthcare.
